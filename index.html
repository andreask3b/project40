Periodise your life like training.

Family, job, health, food, and sport share one week. You cannot max all five at once. Each 4–8 week block has one focus. The others sit on a minimum. Keep about 20% of the week empty for the unplanned.

The rule

Protect each area’s minimum. Raise only one at a time. If sleep slips, work piles up, or readiness trends drift, deload the week — don’t add.

When to get help

Persistent sleep problems, a low mood for weeks, or chest pain or palpitations: seek medical help promptly. This site is not medical advice.

Who it’s for

If your best training window is after dinner, you’re the brief.

Project 40 is for people roughly 35–45 who still care how they look and how they perform — parents, engineers, builders — who won’t (and shouldn’t) live in a gym.
